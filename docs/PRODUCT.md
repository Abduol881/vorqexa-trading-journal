# Product Plan

## Vision

Help traders understand performance, manage risk, and improve decision-making through accurate records and structured reflection.

## MVP

- Account registration, verification, recovery, and logout.
- Manual trade create, read, update, and delete.
- Trade history with filtering, sorting, and pagination.
- Trade fields for market, side, entry/exit, quantity, fees, timestamps, stop loss, take profit, tags, setup, and notes.
- Dashboard metrics with documented definitions.
- Responsive desktop and mobile interface.
- Data export and account deletion.

## Later releases

1. Trading diary, session reviews, screenshots, and risk rules.
2. CSV import and reconciliation.
3. Orderly trade-history integration after official API access is verified.
4. Advanced analytics and multi-account support.
5. Optional AI review and subscription features.

## Product principles

- Manual journaling remains useful even without integrations.
- Explain metric definitions and limitations.
- Never imply that past performance predicts future returns.
- Do not collect wallet seed phrases or private keys.
- Do not make unsupported claims about external API access.
