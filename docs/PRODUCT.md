# Product definition

Vorqexa Journal helps traders record what they traded, why they entered, how they managed risk, and what they learned.

## First release
1. Account creation, sign-in/out, password recovery, and a private workspace.
2. Manual trade create/read/update/delete.
3. Journal notes and user-owned tags.
4. Basic performance metrics calculated from closed trades.
5. CSV import/export with preview, validation, and duplicate handling.
6. Useful loading, empty, error, and success states.

## Later
Optional Orderly import after official API permissions are verified; filters and charts; private screenshots; advanced risk metrics and review routines.

## Non-goals
No trade execution, custody, wallet secrets, required exchange connection, fake real-looking performance, or financial advice.

## Acceptance
The journal works without an exchange; calculations are defined and tested; users can export their data and request account/data deletion; placeholder data is clearly labeled.
